# NOTICE

VNKey is a Vietnamese input method for macOS.

## Derivative work of OpenKey

VNKey is a derivative work of [OpenKey](https://github.com/tuyenvm/OpenKey),
created by Tuyen Mai and contributors. OpenKey is licensed under the
GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0).

VNKey re-implements OpenKey's Vietnamese input engine (key processing,
spelling rules, macro handling, smart switching, and conversion tool) in
Rust, and replaces OpenKey's platform integration with a Tauri v2 shell.
